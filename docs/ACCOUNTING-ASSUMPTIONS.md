# Accounting and forecast assumptions

Only USD, integer safe cents, limited service-business accounts. Every journal line has exactly one positive side; every posted entry balances. Credit note correction: Dr sales returns (contra service revenue), Cr accounts receivable. Matched new receipt: Dr cash, Cr receivables. Duplicate reversal swaps the actual duplicate entry's sides and references that entry; originals remain retained.

The fixture opens with Dr cash / Cr opening equity $20,000. It then records 10 service invoices, one duplicate posting, historical posted receipts, and an explicit dated owner distribution equal to those historical receipts. That distribution is source-backed in the fixture ledger; it keeps the forecast starting cash at the stipulated $20,000 rather than silently double-counting historical receipts. The ledger contains the deliberate duplicate as an error awaiting review. No balance-sheet or profit claims are made from the intentionally messy ledger.

Receipt invoice allocations are explicit supported inputs; fuzzy bank matching is not implemented. Partial payments remain normal facts, not automatic write-offs. Overpayments or excess credits raise exceptions instead of silently clamping. Duplicate ledger representation does not create a second normalized invoice or forecast collection.

Outstanding = normalized invoice amount − approved credits − posted allocated receipts. The original baseline excludes approved credit corrections and scenario changes. Revised includes approved changes and selected explicit assumptions. Credit preview is labeled unapproved. Date scenarios never change posted journal entries or revenue. An amount scenario may collect less than the remaining receivable without asserting a loss; excess is rejected.

Forecast buckets are explicit Monday-start local calendar dates, start-inclusive/end-exclusive. Calendar arithmetic uses UTC date-only integers to avoid DST-hour shifts; business timezone is fixed America/Los_Angeles. First bucket is October 26–November 2, 2026. Overdue expected collections are explicitly listed and allocated to week 1 as an assumption; outside-horizon collections/obligations are listed. Shortfall threshold is zero closing cash, not an intraweek liquidity guarantee. Current obligations total payroll $12,000 each in weeks 1 and 2; studio/software obligations start later.

Exact primary case: opening $20,000 + Invoice A $20,000 − two payrolls $24,000 = $16,000 after week 2. Approved issued credit $8,000 reduces collectable balance to $12,000: closing week 2 $8,000. Moving that remaining collection to week 3 yields −$4,000 after week 2 and $8,000 after week 3. Other unpaid example balances collect outside 13 weeks. Expected manifest is separate in fixtures/expected.json.

Invoice/credit source edits are supported demo inputs, not autonomous edits to real books. Once any proposal is approved, changing imported invoice amount requires reset. Financial statements, tax advice, underwriting, real banking, calibrated probabilities, and real payment execution are out of scope for this implementation.

Newly posted receipts are allocated by their actual transaction dates. A new pre-horizon historical receipt explicitly reconciles opening cash; a receipt within the horizon enters its week; one beyond the horizon is listed outside it. Existing imported receipts are already in the opening snapshot and are not added twice. Money display preserves fractional-dollar cents instead of rounding evidence to whole dollars.
