# Upload-ready business examples

These fictional files demonstrate the new converter using ordinary dollar amounts and business column names. Upload receivables.csv, receipts.csv and bills.csv together at /import. Confirm the records, enter your business name, opening cash and forecast start date, then import.

Invoices establish opening receivable balances. Payments and credits remain pending until investigation and approval. Do not import payments already deducted from a source balance. Original uploads remain attached as sources. CSV, TSV, XLSX/XLS, JSON, text PDFs, DOCX and text are parsed locally. Scans/photos can use one bounded AI extraction when live review is configured. Unknown values require confirmation.
